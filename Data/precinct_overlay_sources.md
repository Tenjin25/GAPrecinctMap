# Dated Georgia precinct overlays

The map uses the precinct boundary vintage for each supported election year. Shapes are matched to precinct result rows by exact name, unique precinct code, reviewed alias, or a conservative unique name match. Unmatched shapes remain uncolored so the map does not invent or duplicate votes.

The 2024 boundary source is the [Georgia General Assembly precinct shapefile](https://www.legis.ga.gov/api/document/docs/default-source/reapportionment-document-library/precincts/gaprec_2024-website-shapefile.zip?sfvrsn=7b38ca99_2). The shapefile archives and election exports for older years were already present in this project. The 2022 official export supplies statewide Gwinnett results that are absent from the project's other 2022 export.

The eight additional 2024 matches are recorded in `precinct_2024_aliases.csv`: Baldwin CTY3 (East Milledgeville), Decatur 513 (Joe L. Sweet Jr. Community Center), Thomas 104/112/116/117 (Coolidge/Merrillville/Patten/Pavo), and Troup 10/11 (Hillcrest/Grace). [Baldwin County's precinct list](https://www.baldwincountyga.com/sites/default/files/fileattachments/board_of_commissioners/page/4396/baldwin_county_november_2021_newsletter.pdf) identifies CTY3 as East Milledgeville at the former Boddie Middle School. [Troup County's 2024 precinct list](https://www.troupcountyga.gov/content/documents/elections/2024/All%20Precincts.pdf) identifies 10 as Hillcrest and 11 as Grace. Thomas codes follow the 2022 dated precinct audit; Decatur 513 is the sole source and shape remainder after all other Decatur precincts match. The corresponding friendly-name overrides display the newer names.

The General Assembly file has 17 Fulton polygons with blank county, FIPS, code, and name fields but a `DISTRICT` value containing the Fulton FIPS and precinct code. The 2024 builder restores those records as Fulton precincts. One combined `SS07D-11C` source polygon is split using the two precinct boundaries in `precinct_2024_fulton_split_reference.geojson`; its small pieces belonging to `SS07C` and `SS11A` are merged into those existing shapes. The reference is extracted from [Fulton County's February 2024 precinct GIS layer](https://services1.arcgis.com/AQDHTHDrZzfsFsB5/ArcGIS/rest/services/VotingDistrictsMapViewerAGOL/FeatureServer/1). Three recovered precincts have distinct election result rows. The other 15 have geometry and block CVAP, but no separate row in the available 2024 election export, so their vote totals remain blank. Fulton County's [official November 2024 statement of votes cast](https://www.fultoncountyga.gov/-/media/2024-Election-Documents/Statement-of-Votes-Cast--Official--Complete--November-5-2024-V2.pdf) and [ballot reconciliation report](https://fultoncountyga.gov/-/media/2024-Election-Documents/Reconciliation-Report-November-5-2024-General-Election.pdf) also omit all 15 codes, although its [election-day polling list](https://fultoncountyga.gov/-/media/Departments/Registration-and-Elections/Fulton-County-Election-Day-Polling-Locations---November-5-2024.pdf) includes many of them. Ten of the shapes receive zero block CVAP; the other five total 39 CVAP. The public records do not establish whether the codes had zero voters or were reported under other precinct codes, so no votes are imputed. Ten other unlabeled source polygons cover military installations and remain excluded.

The project's official 2022 Governor export contains 11 of these 15 codes, each with 0–3 votes; the other four have no 2022 row. This supports treating them as very small precinct fragments but does not establish their November 2024 vote totals. Fulton County [approved precinct boundary consolidations in 2025](https://fulton.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?GUID=4837D134-C0C5-4C66-AB92-46390B2253F3&ID=7370757&Options=&Search=) for precincts with no assigned voters or similar districts. Those changes occurred after the 2024 general election and cannot serve as a 2024 result-code crosswalk. A county election-management-system code crosswalk or a contemporaneous voter-registration precinct assignment report is still needed to resolve the 15 codes.

`precinct_2024_fulton_nov2025_comparison.csv` measures how each unmatched 2024 shape overlaps [Fulton's November 2025 precinct GIS layer](https://services1.arcgis.com/AQDHTHDrZzfsFsB5/ArcGIS/rest/services/Voting_Precincts_Nov_2025/FeatureServer/0), using equal-area projection. Four codes still exist in that later layer (`08F2`, `SC08A`, `UC01C`, `UC033`); the other 11 do not. Every fragment has a largest 2025 overlap of at least 93%, but several cross more than one 2025 precinct. Run `py scripts/compare_fulton_precinct_vintages.py` to refresh the comparison. The November 2025 layer is used for this diagnostic table only; it is not substituted into the 2024 election overlay.

`precinct_2024_fulton_tabblocks.geojson` clips the [2020 Census Fulton County tabblocks](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2020PL/LAYER/TABBLOCK/2020/tl_2020_13121_tabblock20.zip) to these 15 unmatched 2024 precinct fragments. Each piece records its block GEOID, whether the block's Census internal point falls inside the fragment, the full block CVAP allocated there, and the November 2025 precinct containing that point. `precinct_2024_fulton_tabblock_audit.csv` summarizes the assignments by fragment. There are 122 positive-area block intersections and 49 assigned internal points. Their 39 CVAP exactly matches the existing 2024 precinct CVAP aggregate; blocks that cross a boundary are counted only where their internal point falls. The later precinct assignments help examine the consolidations but do not establish where 2024 votes were reported. Regenerate with `py scripts/audit_fulton_unmatched_tabblocks.py --cvap-zip Data/ga_cvap_2024_2020_b_csv.zip` (or supply the local path to that ZIP); the script downloads the Census block archive when absent.

`precinct_<year>_match_audit.csv` records each match method or an unmatched shape. `precinct_2024_aliases.csv` and `precinct_historical_aliases.csv` contain manually reviewed source-name matches. The 2024 Lee, Long, Paulding, and Polk matches can be checked against the county [Lee polling locations](https://www.leecountyga.gov/government/boards/elections-registrations/voting-locations), [Long precinct list](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5f57e563ecdd451953242fc7/t/68bf2455174e3a5a3c44b577/1757357141836/2025_Long_County_Election_Information.pdf), [Paulding precinct list](https://www.paulding.gov/DocumentCenter/View/14780/Paulding-County-Precinct-2025-Official-List), and [Polk precinct list](https://irp.cdn-website.com/a6b5b4c5/files/uploaded/Precincts2024.pdf). `precinct_friendly_names_latest.json` keeps useful county/code to label mappings, preferring the latest boundary name and expanding unambiguous abbreviations such as UMC to United Methodist Church. Matching precinct-number prefixes are removed from display names. `precinct_friendly_overrides.csv` preserves reviewed labels when the files are rebuilt. Case-only repeats are omitted. The dated polygon and centroid files retain friendly labels even when a mapping is omitted.

The historical rebuild combines disconnected source records with the same county and precinct code into one multipart precinct. This resolves duplicate blank pieces in 2012 Fulton, Lumpkin, and Muscogee, 2018 Columbia, and 2022 Chatham without counting their votes twice. `precinct_historical_split_aliases.csv` records source result rows split by city or district but belonging to one boundary. The 2014 and 2018 Ware code/name crosswalk follows the [2011 Georgia House journal's Ware VTD list](https://dlg.usg.edu/record/dlg_ggpd_y-ga-bl404-b2011-bspec-p-belec-p-btext). The 2014 and 2016 Spalding code/name crosswalk follows the [2011 House journal](https://dlg.usg.edu/record/dlg_ggpd_y-ga-bl404-b2011-bspec-p-belec-p-btext) and [2015 House journal](https://www.house.ga.gov/Documents/House%20Journals/2015/Day%2029.pdf).

Every distinct 2012–2022 general-election shape now has a result match. The probe-contest vote totals agree exactly with their precinct exports for 2014, 2016, and 2018. The remaining export rows without a compatible separate boundary are 663 presidential votes in 2012 (Chattooga Cloudland/Teloga and Fulton ML01A/CP05A), one Fulton vote in 2020 (01I), and five votes in 2022 (Habersham City of Baldwin and Fulton SS07D). These rows are not assigned to a nearby shape without a verified boundary crosswalk. The January 2021 Senate runoffs use the 2020 precinct geometry and a separate `precinct_contests_2021.json` produced by `build_2021_runoff_precinct_contests.py`. That builder can reuse exact polling-place names from the November 2020 match audit when a January 2021 name differs from the friendly label. Its 37 unmatched shapes include 30 in Chattooga, Grady, and Greene, whose precinct detail is absent from the available statewide ZIP. Camden's 14 precincts are recovered from its [official January 2021 statement of votes cast](https://www.camdencountyga.gov/DocumentCenter/View/12272/Statement-of-Votes-Cast), transcribed in `20210105__ga__runoff__camden_precinct.csv` and validated against certified county totals for both Senate contests. The other seven unmatched 2021 shapes are in Dougherty and Paulding, where polling-place names and the 2020 boundary labels do not provide a reliable crosswalk. Rockdale code labels are reviewed in `precinct_friendly_overrides.csv` and apply to both dated shapes and latest friendly names.

Different precinct codes may intentionally share a polling-place label. Those separate code mappings are retained.

Precinct CVAP is rebuilt for every dated boundary set in `cvap_aggregates/precinct_shapes_<year>__cvap24.csv`. The source is `ga_cvap_2024_2020_b_csv.zip` on 2020 Census blocks. Each block's Census internal point assigns its full 2020–2024 ACS CVAP estimate to one precinct in the same county; 170–318 blocks per year required nearest-precinct assignment because their points fell outside labeled precinct shapes. This preserves each county's sum of block estimates, but a block divided by a precinct boundary is allocated as one unit. Independently published county CVAP estimates can differ slightly from sums of rounded blocks. The map uses these dated CVAP rows for precinct demographic colors and totals. The legacy 2020 precinct VAP colors are used only with the legacy geometry.

Rebuild with `py scripts/build_2024_precinct_overlay.py`, `py scripts/build_historical_precinct_overlays.py`, `py scripts/build_2021_runoff_precinct_contests.py`, and `py scripts/rebuild_precinct_cvap.py` from the repository root. The 2024 builder needs the General Assembly ZIP at `Data/_external/gaprec_2024-website-shapefile.zip`.
